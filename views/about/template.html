About My Yoga

A short evening yoga sequence for people who'd rather not be working out.

What this is

My Yoga is a guided sixteen-minute evening practice. You tap Begin, set your phone down, and a quiet voice walks you through a sequence of gentle stretches and breathing — forward folds, supine twists, hip openers, neck rolls, a body scan, and child's pose to finish. The screen stays awake the whole time so you don't have to touch it.

It's the same practice every night. That's intentional. The point isn't variety — it's not having to think.

Who it's for

This was built for one specific kind of person: someone who already trains. You lift, you run, you climb, you cycle, you take a hard class three times a week — whatever it is, your day already has its workout in it. You don't need another one. What you need, in the hour before bed, is the opposite of another workout. Something that brings the heart rate down rather than up. Something that asks the body to lengthen and the mind to slow.

That's what this is for.

What it isn't

It is not a workout. There are no standing poses. No Warrior I, II, or III. No Chair pose. No Sun Salutations. No core work, no planks, no chaturanga. Nothing in this sequence is designed to elevate your heart rate, build strength, or burn calories. Anything that would do those things is deliberately left out.

How a session works

Find a quiet corner. A mat is nice but a folded blanket on the carpet works too. Tap Begin. Put the phone where you can hear it but don't have to look at it.

The voice guides you through each pose: how to enter it, how to breathe in it, when to switch sides, when to move on. There's nothing to count, nothing to track, nothing to score.

When the sequence ends, the voice will say so. The screen will fall asleep on its own from there.

Privacy

There is no account. There is no sign-in. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no cookies, no third-party requests of any kind. Nothing about your practice leaves your device. The whole app is HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that runs locally in your browser.

The fonts are bundled with the app rather than loaded from Google's font service, for the same reason: not even a font request gets logged anywhere on your behalf.

Source

My Yoga is open source. The full code, the pose sequence, the narration text — everything — lives in a public GitHub repository. MIT license.
