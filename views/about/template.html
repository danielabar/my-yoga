About My Yoga

A short evening yoga sequence for people who already train and want to wind down well.

What this is

My Yoga is a guided sixteen-minute evening practice. You tap Begin, set your phone down, and a quiet voice walks you through a sequence of gentle stretches and breathing.

Who it's for

This was built for one specific kind of person: someone who already trains. You lift, you run, you climb, you cycle, you take a hard class three times a week — whatever it is, your day already has its workout in it. You don't need another one. What you need, in the hour before bed, is the opposite of another workout. Something that brings the heart rate down rather than up. Something that asks the body to lengthen and the mind to slow.

How a session works

Find a quiet corner. A mat is nice but a folded blanket on the carpet works too. Tap Begin. Put the phone where you can hear it but don't have to look at it.

The voice guides you through each pose: how to enter it, how to breathe in it, when to switch sides, when to move on.

When the sequence ends, the voice will say so. The screen will fall asleep on its own from there.

Source
